fried rice and turkey
we will wash our turkey properly with water and put them into our well rinsed pot and then we add our chopped pepper, diced onions, salt, maggi, curry, thyme, zuri, lassor. we mix that properly and then turn on our gas and put that on it to cook properly. while that's on fire, we rinse our veggies and chop them to our desired sizes. and now, our turkey should be well cooked. we dont want to boil them to become too soft because we are still going to be frying them. so we rinse our frying pan properly and put on the fire to allow the water before adding in our vegetable oil, we wait a lil bit for our vegetable oil to get hot and we put in our turkey to fry one after the other. while our turkey is golden brown and we take them out of the oil, we rince the pot we are going to be using to make our fried rice and place that on fire, we will leave the water to dry and then we add little of the oil we used in frying our turkey. and while that's on fire, we will be adding our washed rice and stir to fry for a lil bit and when that is done, we will be adding our turkey stock and other seasoning to taste better if our turkey stock isnt enough to give it a great taste. we stir that together and add enough water to cook and we cover that to cook. and we proceed to adding little of our leftover oil into a pan to fry our veggies. we dont want to allow them get overcooked because they are veggies and we will add them to our cooked rice on fire and mix properly till they are properly into eachother and our delicious fried rice is ready. serve with our turkey and any refreshing drink of your choice
